How invitations work

  1. Enter the employee's Kannon email.
  2. Choose Viewer, Purchasing, Scheduler, or Administrator.
  3. The employee receives a dashboard invitation.
  4. They sign in with their normal Microsoft 365 account.
  5. The dashboard applies the role you assigned.
